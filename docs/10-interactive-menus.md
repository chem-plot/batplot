## Quick orientation

- Menus are usually **Styles | Geometries | Options** (*operando* adds ***Operando*** / **Side Panel** columns when relevant).
- The **same letter can mean different things by mode** (`c` colors vs CIF vs cycles; `a` rearrange vs capacity/ion vs density vs add-file). Always read the printed row for that mode.
- Overwrite shortcuts (`os` / `oe` / `ops` / `opsg`) appear only after a previous save/export on that figure.

CLI flags are listed in [Summary of flags](12-summary-of-flags.md). Workflows stay in chapters 5–9.
